# Review Rubric — Top Skills and Agents Portfolio

This rubric is the frozen, machine-readable review contract for track
`20260726-top-skills-agents-review`. The canonical verifier
(`scripts/verify_portfolio_review.py`) and the packet schemas
(`schemas/skill-packet.schema.json`, `schemas/agent-packet.schema.json`) encode
these exact stable IDs. Packets are generated by iterating the exact selected
identities and exact rubric IDs; the verifier compares packet IDs as **set
equality** with the frozen rubric — never a count or a self-reported boolean.

Every item requires: `result` (`Pass` | `Fail` | `Not Applicable` |
`Unverified`), `severity` (`Critical` | `Major` | `Minor` | `Optional` |
`Info`), `confidence` (`high` | `medium` | `low`), `source_path`, and
`evidence`. `Pass` requires nonempty command/inspection evidence. `Not
Applicable` and `Unverified` require a nonempty `applicability_reason` /
`blocker_reason`.

The verifier rejects: missing IDs, duplicate IDs, unknown IDs, empty evidence
on a `Pass`, invalid N/A reasons, `Pass` without evidence, and packet
selections that do not exactly equal the frozen 20/10 identities.

## Functional smoke-test safety contract

For skills (Task 2.2) and agents (Task 3.2 / 5.2): disposable fixtures, exact
harness/`opencode run` envelopes, 60/180-second bounds via `run_bounded.py`, one
session, 20 tool calls, and 10K token growth per case. Network and external
mutations are prohibited. If a fixture cannot prove the role without
credentials or external mutation, record `FUNCTIONAL_SMOKE_TEST_UNVERIFIED`
(skill) / `AGENT_SMOKE_UNVERIFIED` (agent) and do not dispatch it. Never send a
message, change a calendar, publish, use credentials, mutate production, or
perform a destructive operation.
